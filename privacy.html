What we collect

If you opt in to texts, we collect your mobile number and any name or email you provide, plus message logs needed to send and receive SMS.

How we use it

We use this information to send the messages you requested, honor STOP/HELP requests, and operate our texting program. We do not sell your mobile number.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers who send messages on our behalf (such as our SMS platform and mobile carriers) and when required by law. SMS opt-in data and consent are not shared with third parties for their own marketing.

Retention

We keep your number while you remain subscribed and as needed for legal or operational records after you opt out.
