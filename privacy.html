SMS Privacy Policy

Frost and Flour

Last updated: September 21, 2026

What we collect

If you opt in to texts, we collect your mobile number and any name or email you provide, plus message logs needed to send and receive SMS.

How we use it

We use this information to send the messages you requested, honor STOP/HELP requests, and operate our texting program. We do not sell your mobile number.

Sharing

Frost and Flour does not sell, rent, trade, or share text messaging originator opt-in data or consent records with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. No mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in information, or consent data will be transferred or shared with any third party, affiliate, or partner for their independent business or marketing purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information won’t be shared with any third parties. Mobile phone numbers, opt-in records, and interaction data are used exclusively to deliver requested text messaging services, process customer support requests, and satisfy applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

Retention

We keep your number while you remain subscribed and as needed for legal or operational records after you opt out.

Your choices

Reply STOP to leave the program. For access or deletion requests, email frostandflourvibes@gmail.com.
